Who We Are
About AG CodeCrafts

AG CodeCrafts is a student-driven initiative based in Puducherry, dedicated to simplifying academic project development. We help students build high-quality projects with ease by offering ready-made and fully customizable solutions across multiple domains.

We specialize in providing projects across Science exhibitions, working models, web development, app development, cybersecurity, IoT, and robotics. Our goal is not just to deliver projects — we ensure every student understands what they present. Simplicity, clarity, and practical implementation — that's our promise.

🎯
Our Mission
Make project development simple, accessible, and stress-free for every student by providing reliable resources, expert guidance, and innovative ideas.
🚀
Our Vision

Innovation First
